# FocusSessionFlow Product Context

## Product

FocusSessionFlow is a personal focus planner for students, freelancers, and knowledge workers. It helps people plan focused work, run timers, capture distractions, and review progress. Group sessions add shared focus rooms through simple invitation links.

## Primary audience

Individuals who want calmer, more consistent focused work, sometimes joined by friends, classmates, or collaborators in a shared session.

## Product personality

Warm, friendly, and motivating. The interface should encourage participation and make group presence feel welcoming without becoming noisy, gamified, or distracting during focus time.

## Design priorities

- Make session state, remaining time, and participant presence immediately understandable.
- Make joining and sharing a group session feel safe, obvious, and low effort.
- Preserve the product's offline-first and privacy-oriented behavior; do not imply server-backed presence when it does not exist.
- Prefer familiar product UI patterns and consistent controls over decorative interaction.

## Accessibility

Target WCAG 2.2 AA, including keyboard access, visible focus, readable contrast, clear status text, and accessible names for participant and sharing controls.

## Visual direction

Use the existing warm-neutral surface system and teal/blue action vocabulary. Keep the visual tone approachable and human through concise copy, soft emphasis, and clear status indicators. Avoid heavy saturation, excessive motion, and game-like progress treatments.

## Scope for this assessment

Assess the group-session list and active-session surfaces for participant synchronization, participant identity, timer semantics/live updates, and in-session link sharing. Report findings before proposing implementation changes.
