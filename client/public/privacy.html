Privacy Policy

Focus Flow · Effective October 16, 2026

At a glance: Focus Flow is a focus-timer and group-session application. You can use personal focus features without signing in. Your personal timers, distraction notes, and reviews are stored in your own browser. If you choose Google sign-in, Focus Flow uses Neon Auth to manage your account and enable connected group sessions.

1. Data that stays on your device

Focus Flow stores personal focus sessions, session templates, distraction notes, daily reviews, preferences and guest group-session history in browser storage, including IndexedDB and localStorage. These records are not automatically uploaded to Neon when you sign in. Export, import and clear tools are available from the application's Settings. Clearing browser data or switching browsers may remove local history unless you exported it.

2. Google sign-in and account data

If you choose “Continue with Google”, Google's authentication service lets Neon Auth receive basic profile information needed for your account, such as a Google account identifier, email address, display name, and profile image when available. Focus Flow uses this information to sign you in, maintain your session and connect you to group-session features. We do not request access to Google Drive, Gmail, Calendar, contacts or other private Google services. Authentication information and session records are processed and stored by Neon Auth. Google also processes data under Google's own privacy practices.

3. Connected group sessions

When signed-in users create or join connected group sessions, Neon stores room details such as title, shared objective, schedule, duration, an optional meeting link, organizer name, display names, member identifiers and join/presence timestamps. Members of a connected room can see its shared information and participant names. Personal distraction notes, individual focus logs and private reviews are not copied into connected group rooms.

Group-session records may remain in the database until the room is removed or a deletion request is handled. The organizer can delete a room using the application.

4. Analytics and visitor counter

Focus Flow includes Vercel Analytics for aggregate traffic information. The public homepage also uses a visitor counter that stores a random visitor identifier in your browser and sends that identifier plus the Focus Flow app identifier to the counter endpoint at dev2stage.vercel.app. Basic technical request metadata may be processed by hosting providers when delivering these features. You can clear the visitor identifier by deleting site storage in your browser.

5. Hosting, security and sharing

Focus Flow is hosted through Vercel; connected account and collaboration data is processed by Neon, with Google involved when you elect to sign in. These providers process information to supply the service. Focus Flow does not include in-app advertising or a feature for selling personal data. Connected-room invitations should be shared only with people you intend to invite; invite holders may see shared room details.

6. Your choices and data requests

You may continue as a guest, sign out of your account, clear your local data from Settings, or export it before clearing. For account-data access or deletion requests, contact the project maintainer through the Focus Flow GitHub repository. The application does not currently offer a self-service account-deletion button. Do not include passwords, private tokens or sensitive information in a public GitHub issue.

7. Policy changes

As the application develops, this notice may be updated to accurately describe its features and data handling. The current version will be made available at this page.
