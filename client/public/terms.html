Focus Flow · Effective October 16, 2026

Summary: Focus Flow is a free, early-stage focus and collaboration tool. Personal features are available as a guest. Google sign-in is optional for connected collaboration. Use the application responsibly and back up anything important to you.

1. About the service

Focus Flow helps you plan focus sessions, keep a timer, capture distractions, review progress and optionally collaborate in group sessions. Features may evolve or become unavailable while this early-stage service is being developed.

2. Guest and account usage

You may use personal features without an account. For connected group sessions, Google authentication may be required. You are responsible for maintaining access to your Google account and any local backups you choose to export. Your browser-local data is not automatically transferred to another device by signing in.

3. Group collaboration

Only share group invitation links and optional meeting URLs with intended participants. Do not post abusive or unlawful content in group titles, messages or display names. Keep other participants' privacy in mind. The room organizer may end or delete a connected room.

4. Availability and data

Focus Flow is provided on an as-available basis. Network features may fail, and browser-local data may be lost through browser resets, privacy settings or device problems. Export data you need to preserve. No uninterrupted operation or storage backup is guaranteed.

5. Third-party services

Google sign-in is provided through Google and Neon Auth. Hosting and optional analytics are provided through Vercel. Your use of these features may also be subject to the respective providers' terms and privacy notices.

6. Changes and questions

Features and these terms may be updated. Material service changes will be reflected in the application or on this page. For questions, use the Focus Flow project repository; never post passwords or private tokens in public issues. See our Privacy Policy for data handling details.
